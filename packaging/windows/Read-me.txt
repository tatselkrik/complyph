ComplyPH 0.12.0 - Initial checks across seven domains

Close ComplyPH and any earlier review-app windows before installing. Open ComplyPH from
its shortcut. Normal use needs no command prompt, browser, Rust, Python, AI model,
API key or subscription.

Inspect document text and findings side by side with independent scrolling.
Click a finding title or Show passage to jump to its saved PDF page or Word
paragraph/table location and highlight its text. Search filters are cleared on a
passage jump. Identical matches are identified rather than silently guessed.

Open supporting provision offline opens the complete article from the exact local
source snapshot recorded by the review, with its quoted text highlighted. Related
provisions and the full saved source are available in the same offline viewer.
These controls do not open a browser or request an online source.

Reports > Open connected review restores a saved review beside its document.
If the original differs from that review, passage navigation is disabled; saved
quotes and the matching historical legal source remain available. A missing or
mismatched legal snapshot is reported without substituting the latest version.

Use Document review notes to record notes for a saved review. Each finding has
Reviewer notes and status: Unresolved, Acknowledged or Resolved. Enter a reviewer
name and note/reason, then Save reviewer update. Names are self-reported, not
verified identities or signatures. Earlier entries remain visible; corrections are
new notes. Resolved is a follow-up label and never changes the automated result.
A new review has its own tracking history.

Export PDF saves the original findings, scope, evidence, legal quotes, identifiers
and complete saved history to a new local file. Save pending notes first. No
browser, printer or internet is needed. Exports contain private excerpts and notes;
store/share them deliberately. Lato fonts are embedded; unsupported scripts cause
an explicit error. Copy full report JSON is the alternative for unsupported text
or reports above 200 pages. Exports do not recheck current originals or laws.

Read PDF/DOCX text locally, compare it with the original, then choose Check document.
Confirm scope for normal daily working hours, ordinary-day overtime additional
premium, night shift differential, regular meal periods and consecutive weekly rest.
For service incentive leave, establish employee coverage, qualifying service and
exceptions separately, and record the review period and eligibility basis. Only
then compare explicit annual paid SIL days with five. Unsupported wording, unpaid
or pooled leave and uncertain eligibility stay unresolved. Open Article 95 and
Book III Rule V offline for eligibility, exceptions and other leave provisions.
Existing reports keep their original findings. Results include passages, source
and document hashes and unresolved states. These twenty numeric comparisons are not
legal certification or a compliance verdict.
No Philippine lawyer has certified the rules. Coverage is limited to the initial checklist; full-domain and payroll/remittance assessments remain future work.

Samples includes seven fictional reader cases, three earlier check fixtures and five SIL cases.
All are test data, not legal templates. Reader limits: 32 MiB, 300 PDF pages,
4 MiB text and a 20-second worker timeout. Run local OCR recognizes up to 20 PDF pages offline, with a 120-second limit.
Inspect page text and low-confidence flags, then separately confirm every OCR
page/number against the original before checking. The runtime is bundled. Images,
text boxes and some revision/layout information may be missing; compare originals.

The local library has 19 preserved references: 14 verified agency downloads,
3 identity-checked uploads and 2 provisional third-party copies. Uploaded DOLE
files have not been byte-compared with agency originals. Pag-IBIG Circular 499
comes from stitched third-party images. OCR figures/matrices need page comparison.
The PwC January 2020 tax compilation is a dated external citation, not bundled.

Legal library > Add legal source imports a local PDF/DOCX reference with its title,
number, agency, official HTTPS citation, domains, dates and verification notes.
Leave unknown dates blank. Imports default to Awaiting verification. Identity
checked by me requires a recorded comparison with the official publication and
does not mean verified agency download. URLs are not fetched. Adding a reference
does not add checking rules. Changed files under the same URL retain earlier
snapshots; identical files retain their original metadata. Scans need external OCR.

Windows workspace: C:\ProgramData\ComplyPH (uses the system ProgramData location).
A single detected earlier LocalAppData workspace is copied and
validated automatically. The old folder remains as backup. References, cached text
and preferences are retained; original documents stay where they are. For custom
or ambiguous locations use the workspace folder picker. Settings can import an
old workspace only into an empty destination; existing data is preserved.

This fixed path is restricted to its owning account, SYSTEM and Administrators;
it is not a shared multi-user store. SQLite is not encrypted by the app. Removing
a document entry deletes its cache, reports and tracking history, preserving the original file.
Uninstall preserves active data, backups and original documents.

The desktop has no HTTP client, AI or document upload feature. The separate
AppContainer updater checks approved agency URLs only. Settings > Watched
document folder enables local, nonrecursive intake after a quiet interval.
Folder intake adds entries only; it never runs OCR/reviews automatically.
Pause or forget intake from Settings. Its local path/hash ledger persists across
restarts; forgetting it clears intake history. Originals remain in place. Report-copy actions
place private report text on your clipboard only when requested.

This Windows build is unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen.

Source updates: open a reference > Source updates and downloaded history.
Review the official URL and choose Approve daily checks. No URL is enabled by
default. Use Check approved sources now for an immediate batch. Daily checks
run while the app is open; closing the app stops its updater.

Downloaded changes remain separate, hash-checked copies awaiting review. Use
Update review needed to filter the library, save a copy and record notes.
Earlier references and report citations are retained. A byte change may be a
webpage layout change and does not automatically change checking rules.

Initial rule pack: 20 checks across seven domains. The fourteen added checks cover
standard probationary duration, worker OSH seminar hours and required PPE charges,
reportable-breach notification time limits, an annual taxable-compensation band,
and stated SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG rates and base limits. Expand the relevant domain
in the scope form. Confirm coverage, review amendments/exceptions, enter a date
within the period under review and record the factual basis. The initial new
profiles support periods through CY2026; this is not a legal expiry date.

SSS salary credit, PhilHealth basic salary and Pag-IBIG fund salary are distinct.
Their bases are not inferred from gross/net pay. The app does not calculate actual
taxes, contributions or remittances, classify an incident, notify the NPC, decide
regularization or establish actual training/PPE provision. Unknown/unsupported
facts stay unresolved. Only one Pag-IBIG employee salary band may be covered.

Five cross-domain Word/PDF cases are included in Samples. Each new finding saves
its own public source hashes and offline section locator. Old reports retain their
original findings; ProgramData storage, source-update isolation and private data
are preserved. No AI review is included.

Library maintenance
Open Legal library > Maintain library to edit version-bound metadata and identity
decisions, compare source versions, adopt/restore reviewed copies and record rule
revalidation. Each decision requires a reviewer, date and basis. Changed support
keeps affected checks held; adopting a reference never rewrites numeric rules.
Earlier snapshots, reports and source citations remain available.

Discover official issuances inventories links from explicitly approved publication
pages. Newly seen links are not automatically downloaded or verified as new laws.
Use Add downloaded legal reference or Assess rule impact to continue deliberately.
The initial twenty checks retain their supported periods; qualified legal review
and broader coverage remain separate. See Library-maintenance.md for limits and
workflow instructions. Private schema 5 and ProgramData storage remain unchanged;
public schema 5 preserves previous catalog data while adding maintenance history.
