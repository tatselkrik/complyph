## Document revision comparisons

ComplyPH now retains extracted readings for document versions and connects revision comparison to each version's own saved reviews and follow-up history.

- Add a revised PDF/DOCX or explicitly link an existing document through **Version history / compare…**. Reading a changed file at the same path also retains a new version.
- Compare highlighted text changes and their page/paragraph locations, read complete retained texts, and jump from a finding to its saved evidence.
- Compare selected review outcomes and separate follow-up statuses. Scope/source/rule/reading differences are visible; missing clauses and omitted checks never count as a correction. Earlier resolved statuses are not copied.
- Open a saved review beside its exact retained reading even after the original moves or changes, and follow its original legal provision offline.
- Reuse an earlier scope only as an explicit starting point, with fresh text/OCR confirmations before a new review.
- Export a comparison PDF with both selected review histories, or full JSON including both retained texts. Existing files are never overwritten.

Private schema 5 migrates to 6 while preserving existing cached/report/note bytes, document references and folder settings. The latest valid old cache can be retained; old reviews without an exact reading association remain labelled, and missing old text is not reconstructed. Original PDF/DOCX bytes stay in their original files.

**C:\ProgramData\ComplyPH**, public schema 5, the nineteen-reference starter collection and the twenty focused checks remain unchanged. Comparisons and exports stay local and do not open or modify the public legal catalog. AI review is not included.
