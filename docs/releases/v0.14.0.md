## Document reading quality

ComplyPH now reads supported difficult layouts more clearly and records the quality and method of each reading. Processing remains local.

- Spatial PDF reading orders supported paragraphs and two separated columns by character positions, joins supported soft wraps, preserves Unicode font mappings and keeps physical page numbers.
- Word text boxes have their own paragraph/table locations. Matching alternative representations are deduplicated; differing alternatives and unread embedded content require manual review.
- **OCR options** adds clockwise rotation, automatic/one-block/scattered layouts, faint-scan contrast enhancement and bounded mild-skew correction. No words or numbers are automatically repaired, and original PDFs are unchanged.
- Visible reading warnings keep unresolved tables, font/image omissions, forms/annotations, poor recognition and other unsupported content at **Needs review**. Text availability and recognition confidence do not establish accuracy or compliance.
- Reading settings, warnings and per-page processing details are retained with exact readings and included in local report exports. Later reruns preserve earlier assessments and their quality context. A changed reading method is distinguished from a document edit.

This completes the initial supported reading-quality milestone under roadmap **#3**, alongside existing local OCR and watched-folder intake. Twelve fictional fixtures exercise eight ordinary-reader and six OCR cases. Broader layouts/scripts, legal review, accessibility and Windows display/device validation remain separate quality work.

The twenty focused checks and nineteen-reference legal collection are unchanged. **C:\ProgramData\ComplyPH**, private schema 6, public schema 5, notes/status history and source-updater isolation remain in place. Earlier retained reading fingerprints and original JSON records are preserved; older absent OCR options stay unknown. An explicit rerun creates a current reading. AI review is not included.

Windows release gates include actual installed layout/OCR workflows and an upgrade from the hash-pinned released v0.13.0 application, verifying old OCR cache reuse, preserved reading/report/note bytes and a new-method rerun without replacing historical quality context. Earlier migration and source-maintenance tests remain required.

Close ComplyPH and run **ComplyPH-Setup-0.14.0.exe**, or extract the complete **ComplyPH-Windows-x64-0.14.0.zip** and open **ComplyPH.exe**. Keep the bundled OCR folder. Ordinary use requires no command prompt, account, AI model or subscription. The unsigned build may trigger SmartScreen.
