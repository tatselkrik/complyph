# ComplyPH 0.4.1 — Connected review

Inspect document text beside its findings and open the complete supporting legal provision offline.

- Add independently scrolling document and findings panes. Click a finding or its evidence location to jump to the saved page/paragraph/table passage and highlight its text.
- Handle whitespace, Unicode, long display chunks and repeated matches. Clear conflicting searches on a passage jump; unmatched evidence is never guessed.
- Open full supporting articles, related provisions and the night-shift implementing rule from the exact source version used by the review. A newer source never silently replaces a historical citation.
- Reopen saved reports in the connected view. Changed or missing originals disable passage navigation while preserving saved evidence and legal references.
- Refresh product documentation, installation/build instructions and source assessment notes. Retire obsolete samples and preview media.
- Update reference-bundle metadata without changing the 19 preserved publication/text payloads, source hashes, provenance classes or rule thresholds. Existing catalogs retain earlier bundles and snapshots.

Close ComplyPH before installing the update. The installer retains the registered installation directory and preserves the ProgramData workspace, earlier data backups and original documents. The portable ZIP can be extracted and opened directly.

The checks remain limited to three labour numeric comparisons. Daily source updates, manual legal-source import, watched folders and in-app OCR remain planned. Windows binaries are unsigned. Machine-readable package/install evidence and checksums accompany the release.
