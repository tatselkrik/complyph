## Local OCR and folder intake

Includes the local OCR and watched-folder features introduced in 0.9.0, with a packaging fix for fresh Windows PCs.

- Recognize up to 20 scanned PDF pages offline with bundled English OCR, physical page references and low-confidence flags. Confirm OCR accuracy separately before running numeric checks.
- Enable optional local PDF/DOCX folder intake with quiet-write checks, duplicate suppression and pause/forget controls. Intake does not start OCR or reviews automatically.
- Link the JPEG codec statically so OCR does not depend on a separately installed Visual C++ runtime. Verify every bundled DLL's import dependencies during preparation and release publication, in addition to checksums and installed workflow tests.

The five labour comparisons, legal reference bundle, private schema 5 and `C:\ProgramData\ComplyPH` workspace are unchanged. Existing documents, reviews, notes and originals are preserved. OCR confidence and human confirmation do not certify accuracy or legal compliance.

Close ComplyPH and install `ComplyPH-Setup-0.9.1.exe`, or extract the complete `ComplyPH-Windows-x64-0.9.1.zip` folder and open `ComplyPH.exe`. Keep the bundled `OCR` folder. No OCR service, subscription, document upload or setup command is required. The build remains unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen.

See [OCR and folder controls, limits and privacy](https://github.com/tatselkrik/complyph/blob/v0.9.1/docs/OCR_AND_FOLDERS.md). `windows-install-check.json`, `release-manifest.json` and checksums identify the exact tested packages/source. Interactive Windows scaling/accessibility remain target-device checks.
