ADVISORY          No. 2026-0050




Electronic Premium Remittance
System (EPRS) Version 4.9.0
Signed September 17, 2026



In line with PhilHealth’s continuing implementation of the
Electronic Premium Remittance System (EPRS) to facilitate
the online remittance, reporting and immediate posting of
premium contributions, all employers in the Government
and Private Sectors are hereby informed that the enhanced
features of the EPRS Version 4.9.0 to support additional
premium payment transactions will be made available
beginning October 1, 2026.

In view thereof, starting September 15, 2026, employers
may submit request letters to their servicing Local Health
Insurance Office (LHIO) or the nearest PhilHealth Regional
Office (PRO) to access the additional payment modules of the
EPRS Version 4.9.0. Please be advised that the enhancement
will only be accessible after processing of the approved
request.

All payments for additional employees and salary differentials
shall be made through the EPRS online payment channels
using the Statement of Premium Account (SPA). Over the
counter payments will no longer be accepted in PhilHealth
Cashiers and Accredited Collecting Agents. Employers are
encouraged to coordinate with their respective PhilHealth
Accounts Information Management Specialist (PAIMS) for
further information on the requirements and procedures for
submission of requests.
