The raw bundle preserves the preserved source bytes. OCR text for D.O. 252-25 and Circular 499 was prepared externally during development; this does not add OCR to the installed app. OSHS was split during inspection because the private reader has a 300-page limit; its full original and derived text are available in the library. The preparation page map records original physical page indices, not a promise that printed footers match every page.

Raw/reading-text SHA-256 values and source-specific notes are in `starter/manifest.json`. No source is promoted to an agency download merely because it passes a checksum. The PwC file is represented only by an external citation and does not count among the 19 snapshots.
